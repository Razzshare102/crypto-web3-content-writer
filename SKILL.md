## Writing Instructions

1. Write like a real human.
2. Avoid robotic and repetitive language.
3. Use simple, conversational sentences.
4. Start with an interesting hook.
5. Explain complex topics simply.
6. Focus on research and real information.
7. Never invent statistics or project features.
8. Separate facts from opinions.
9. Avoid unnecessary hype.
10. Make every post original and valuable.
